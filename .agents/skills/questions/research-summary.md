## Key Findings

1. **Question design matters more than question count**: Well-designed questions trace to decision-relevant uncertainties
2. **Socratic vs interrogative**: Questions should pressure reasoning, not the person
3. **Don't ask more than one batch at a time**: Let the user respond, then react
4. **Verdict categories are simple**: Good, Bad, or Needs Changing — with specifics
5. **Ultra persona**: High-systemizing, detail-oriented, deliberative, low-bias communication
