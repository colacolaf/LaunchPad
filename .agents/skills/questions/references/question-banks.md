# /questions — Question Banks

## How to Use

Select the subject type, then pick the most relevant questions. Don't ask them all — pick the 3-12 most decision-relevant ones based on context.

---

## Business / Startup

**Core questions (Step 1):**
1. What's the problem you're solving and how do you know people have it?
2. Who's the customer and how will you reach them?
3. What's the business model — how do you make money?
4. Who are your competitors and why will you win?
5. What's the single biggest risk and why are you confident it's manageable?

**Detail questions (Step 2):**
6. What are your unit economics (CAC, LTV, margins)?
7. What's the market size — TAM, SAM, SOM?
8. What's the development timeline and key milestones?
9. What's the team's relevant experience?
10. What's the go-to-market strategy — channels, cost, timeline?
11. What are the fixed vs variable costs?
12. What's the regulatory or legal landscape?
13. What's the technology stack and are there patent/IP considerations?
14. What's the revenue model — subscription, transaction, advertising?
15. What's the customer acquisition cost at launch vs at scale?

## Software / Product

**Core questions:**
1. What's the core user need and who validated it?
2. What's the architecture and what are the hardest technical problems?
3. What's the MVP scope and what gets cut if time runs short?
4. What's the user experience — how does a first-time user succeed?
5. What's the deployment and maintenance plan?

**Detail questions:**
6. What dependencies exist — libraries, services, APIs?
7. What's the scaling plan — at what user count does it break?
8. What's the security model — authentication, authorization, data protection?
9. What's the testing strategy — unit, integration, E2E?
10. What's the data model and storage strategy?
11. What's the performance budget and worst-case latency?
12. What's the rollback plan if a deployment goes wrong?
13. What's the monitoring and observability strategy?
14. What happens when a third-party dependency fails or changes?
15. What's the localization/i18n plan?

## Purchase (Significant)

**Core questions:**
1. What's the total cost including maintenance/accessories/subscriptions?
2. What alternatives did you consider and why did you reject them?
3. What's the real-world experience — not specs, but actual use?
4. What are the common failure modes or complaints?
5. What's the resale value or exit strategy?

**Detail questions:**
6. What's the warranty and what does it NOT cover?
7. What's the total cost of ownership over 3-5 years?
8. What's the reliability track record — what's the failure rate?
9. What's the user community like — active, helpful, frustrated?
10. What are the consumables cost (if applicable)?
11. What's the availability — can you get it now or wait?
12. What's the compatibility with existing equipment/ecosystem?
13. What's the learning curve — how long to be productive with it?
14. What's the upgrade path and expected lifespan?
15. What's the return policy and how painful is it to exercise?

## Life Decision

**Core questions:**
1. What are the best-case, worst-case, and most-likely outcomes?
2. What's reversible and what isn't?
3. What's the cost (financial, time, emotional) and can you afford it?
4. What would you advise a close friend in the same situation?
5. What does your support network think?

**Detail questions:**
6. What's the opportunity cost — what are you saying no to?
7. What's the timeline — when do you need to decide and what's the cost of delay?
8. What's your fallback plan if it doesn't work out?
9. Have you talked to someone who did this and regretted it?
10. What's the impact on your closest relationships?
11. What changes about your daily life if you do this?
12. What do you know now that you wish you'd known before considering this?
13. What's the financial runway and what happens if it runs out?
14. What's the emotional resilience required and do you have the support?
15. What would "success" look like in 1 year, 3 years, 5 years?

## Career Move

**Core questions:**
1. What's the growth trajectory — where does this lead in 2-5 years?
2. What's the comp trajectory — base, upside, ceiling?
3. What's the culture and management quality — do you know?
4. What's the work-life balance reality — not the pitch, but reality?
5. What's the opportunity cost of taking this vs staying/looking longer?

**Detail questions:**
6. What's the manager's track record — have they developed people?
7. What's the team dynamic — who would you work with day-to-day?
8. What's the company's financial health — funded, profitable, burning?
9. What's the role scope — will it grow or is it well-defined?
10. What's the learning potential — new skills, domain, network?
11. What's the commute/remote flexibility?
12. What do former employees say about the company?
13. What's the promotion track and timeline?
14. What's the downside scenario — layoffs, reorgs, pivots?
15. What would make you stay in your current role?

## Research / Scientific Claim

**Core questions:**
1. What's the claim and is it falsifiable?
2. What's the evidence strength — primary data, sample size, methodology?
3. Are there alternative explanations for the findings?
4. Has this been replicated and by whom?
5. What do critics say and are their objections substantive?

**Detail questions:**
6. What's the sample size and is it representative?
7. What's the effect size — is it practically significant or just statistically significant?
8. What's the methodology — randomized, controlled, blinded?
9. What are the confounds — what else could explain the results?
10. Where was it published and what's the journal's reputation?
11. Has there been a meta-analysis on this topic?
12. What's the funding source and potential conflicts of interest?
13. What's the replication record — has anyone tried and failed?
14. What are the boundary conditions — when does it NOT hold?
15. What's the consensus in the field — agreement, disagreement, or no consensus?
