# Research: IMC Prosperity

> **Site:** prosperity.imc.com · **Organizer:** IMC (global research-driven quantitative trading firm, since 1989) · **Verified:** Aug 21, 2026 (site fetch)

## What it is

IMC's flagship online trading challenge. A **five-round trading challenge** in which students combine **algorithmic and manual trading strategies** to maximize profit, grow their trading outpost, and climb a global leaderboard.

## Verified facts (Prosperity 4, the 2026 edition)

- **18,803 teams** participated — matches the plan's figure.
- **$50,000 USD** prize pool shared among winners.
- Designed for **university students** worldwide; open to students willing to commit **an hour or two a day**.
- Mix of algorithmic trading (code) + manual challenges.
- The more teams invest in their algorithm and manual challenges, the higher they climb.

## What to steal vs. what to skip

**Steal:**
- **The competition model:** rounds, a leaderboard, a mix of algorithmic + manual participation, a clear prize/incentive structure. This is the template for our VENUE competition (`docs/venue.md`).
- **The "learn by playing" framing** — how they make trading accessible and keep engagement high.
- **Team dynamics:** collective intelligence is core to the experience — relevant when we recruit ≥10 traders.

**Skip:**
- The closed platform — we host our own venue.
- Prize eligibility specifics: **prizes are university-restricted** (the plan flags this — verify before relying on any prize, since it likely doesn't apply to a high-school participant).
