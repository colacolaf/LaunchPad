# Internship Playbook — the "build it for free" lever

> The core insight: nobody hires a 16-year-old, but some firms will accept a free, impressive, self-contained build. So you never pitch "I want an internship" — you pitch "I built X; I'll build you Y for free as a case study." The project converts employment friction (HR, liability, labor laws) into a no-friction offer (a tool appears in their inbox).

## What you're selling (the artifact kit)

1. **GitHub repo** — README with benchmarks + methodology + live CI badge
2. **2-minute demo video** — matching engine running, latency numbers live
3. **One-page write-up** — what / why / results, honest limits
4. **The hosted competition** — proof people used it
5. **(Later) the paper draft**

## Target list (cold email / LinkedIn, in order of realism)

- Local RIAs / independent wealth managers / financial advisors
- CPA and accounting firms (finance-adjacent, often small, often friendly)
- Small fintech / finance startups (founders answer their own email)
- Local credit unions, community banks, insurance agencies
- Family offices (via any warm connection)
- Professors / alumni networks / parents' network (warm beats cold)
- The team competition advisor + team parents

## Cold email template (≤10 lines, send 20+)

```
Subject: Built a trading exchange at 16 — free tool for your firm?

Hi [Name],

I'm a junior who builds finance software. I built a matching engine + market
simulator — [1-line what it does, link to repo] — and I'm running a paper-trading
competition on it this spring.

I'm looking for a free, project-based internship at a firm like yours: I'd build
you a small tool pro bono (e.g., [specific: a portfolio review dashboard / client
report generator / market-data digest]) as a case study — you get the tool, I get
real-world feedback and a reference.

Worth a 15-minute call? [link to repo]
```

## Realistic expectations

- Expect a **~5–10% reply rate** and mostly "no"s — liability rules and HR friction kill most unpaid-minor setups regardless of skill.
- Every "no" is free market research: ask why and adapt the offer.
- Target **1–3 wins by summer 2027**. A "win" can be a 2-week project collaboration, a reference, a mentor, or a real unpaid internship.
- **Pre-commit to 20 sends** before judging the approach.

## Compliance note (verify before committing)

Unpaid work for a business can still implicate state/federal labor laws for minors. The safe framing is **independent project collaboration** (you build, they give feedback/reference), not "employment." Involve parents in any arrangement. Verify your state's rules first.

## Anti-patterns

- Never claim results you don't have (the record and benchmarks are the ceiling of what you can say).
- Never pitch "hire me" — pitch "here's a tool I'll build you for free."
- Never present the paper as finished before it exists.
