## How to add an entry

- Date = today.
- Decision = the thing you chose or did (one line).
- Rationale = why (one line, honest).
- Status = Adopted / Confirm at [phase] / Rejected / Deferred.
- Chronological order, newest at the bottom — one table, no rows outside it.
